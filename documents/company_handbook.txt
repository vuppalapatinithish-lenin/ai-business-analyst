COMPANY EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 18 annual leave days per year.

Employees can apply for casual leave after completing three months of employment.

Employees can request sick leave when they are unable to work due to illness.

WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees can work from home up to two days per week with manager approval.

Employees must remain available during normal working hours while working from home.

WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to attend scheduled meetings during working hours.

PERFORMANCE POLICY

Employee performance is reviewed twice every year.

Performance reviews consider project contributions, teamwork, communication, problem solving, and overall work quality.

TRAINING POLICY

New employees receive an onboarding program during their first month.

Employees can participate in technical and professional development programs approved by their manager.
